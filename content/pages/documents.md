Printable guides to help your household prepare. These links open PDF files on the publishing organizations’ websites, where you can save or print them.

## Pack a go-bag

**City of Berkeley — Evacuation Planning Worksheets**

Includes a go-bag packing checklist, with items to pack ahead and items to add when leaving. It also helps you plan evacuation routes and household arrangements. The go-bag checklist is on PDF page 10 in the screen-reader-friendly section.

17 pages. Updated May 2026. For paper copies, the City recommends printing pages 2–7; pages 8–17 provide the accessible version.

[Open Berkeley’s go-bag and evacuation planning worksheets (PDF)](https://berkeleyca.gov/sites/default/files/2022-02/berkeley-fire-weather-planning-tool.pdf)

## General emergency preparedness checklist

**American Red Cross — Be Red Cross Ready: Preparedness Checklist**

A simple two-page overview covering emergency supplies, household plans, staying informed, and reconnecting with loved ones. Includes both go-kit and stay-at-home supplies.

[Open the Red Cross preparedness checklist (PDF)](https://www.redcross.org/content/dam/redcross/get-help/pdfs/preparedness-essentials/Preparedness-Checklist-Update_ENG.pdf)
