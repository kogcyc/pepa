Title: Maps
Slug: maps

Explore Berkeley’s official maps for wildfire planning and emergency information.

## Fire hazard zones

The City of Berkeley’s Community GIS Portal includes Fire Hazard Severity Zones and other wildfire information. Open the **Fire and Emergency Services** map view to explore these layers.

Fire hazard zones describe longer-term hazards. They do not show current evacuation orders.

[Open Berkeley’s Community GIS Portal](https://berkeleyca.gov/city-services/community-gis-portal)

## Emergency evacuation map

Berkeley’s Emergency Map provides current information about evacuation orders and other protective actions. Look up an address to check the status for your home or another important location.

The City’s guide below links to the live map and explains how to use it.

[Open Berkeley’s emergency map guide and live map link](https://berkeleyca.gov/community-recreation/news/use-citys-emergency-map-critical-info-trusted-updates)
