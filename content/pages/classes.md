Title: Classes
Slug: classes

Find preparedness classes offered by the City of Berkeley and other organizations. Follow the provider links below for current dates, availability, prerequisites, fees, and registration.

## City of Berkeley — Berkeley Ready

Start with practical household preparedness training. Topics include making a disaster plan, evacuation planning and go-bags, and planning for fire weather.

[Explore Berkeley Ready classes and registration](https://berkeleyca.gov/safety-health/disaster-preparedness/trainings)

## City of Berkeley — CERT

Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) training builds skills in disaster preparedness, team organization, disaster medical operations, disaster mental health, fire safety and utilities, and light search and rescue. Some units have prerequisites.

[View Berkeley CERT training details and upcoming classes](https://berkeleyca.gov/safety-health/disaster-preparedness/trainings)

## American Red Cross

Find first aid, CPR, and AED courses. Search by city or ZIP code for nearby options, or explore online classes. Course format and certification vary by class.

[Find Red Cross classes](https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class)

## Berkeley Disaster Preparedness Neighborhood Network

Explore neighborhood preparedness training resources, recorded presentations, and links to CERT information from the Berkeley Disaster Preparedness Neighborhood Network (BDPNN).

[Explore BDPNN training resources](https://www.bdpnn.org/resources/training-cert-classes)

Register directly with the organization offering the course. Check its website for the latest information before making plans.
